Privacy

Last updated October 7, 2026

What I collect

  • When you send a booking request: your name, email, phone number, preferred day, the details of the tattoo you want, and any photos you upload (references, the placement area, your pet).
  • Nothing else. This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising.

Where it goes

  • The form is delivered to my inbox by FormSubmit, which forwards it by email and does not keep a copy beyond delivery.
  • The booking page uses Google reCAPTCHA to block spam. It loads only once you start filling in the form, and Google may collect device information and set cookies under the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • I never sell or share your details, and I don't use your photos for anything other than planning your tattoo.

How long I keep it

  • As long as it takes to arrange and complete your tattoo, and for follow-up questions about healing.
  • Want it deleted sooner? Message me on Instagram and I'll remove your request and photos.
